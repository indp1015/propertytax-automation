This share is modest when juxtaposed with the national average property‑tax‑to‑income ratio, which typically exceeds 2 percent in many jurisdictions. The 1.31 percent share suggests that the tax component is financially sustainable for the median household, leaving ample income for other essential expenses and discretionary spending.
