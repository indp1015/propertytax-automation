While this proportion remains modest, it represents a recurring expense that must be factored into household budgeting. A concrete insight emerges from the data: despite a lower median tax amount than the national median, Elk County’s effective rate is more than double the state average, indicating that the tax system is structured to generate revenue from a relatively small property base, potentially placing a disproportionate burden on lower‑valued properties.
