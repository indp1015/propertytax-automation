Property Tax in Linn County, Iowa

In Linn County, Iowa, the median property tax is $3,547/year — an effective tax rate of 1.66%. The median home value is $214,300.

Market Analysis

Linn County's effective property tax rate of 1.66% is 26.4% above the Iowa state average and 81.2% above the U.S. national average. At this rate, a homeowner with a $400,000 property would pay approximately $6,640 per year in property taxes.
