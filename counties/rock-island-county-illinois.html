Market Analysis

Rock Island County's effective property tax rate of 2.28% is 26.9% above the Illinois state average and 148.9% above the U.S. national average. At this rate, a homeowner with a $400,000 property would pay approximately $9,119 per year in property taxes.

📝 Property Tax Notes

The effective property‑tax rate in Rock Island County stands at 2.28 %, which is more than double the national average of 0.92 % and exceeds the Illinois state average by 2.28 % points (the state figure is listed as 0.00 % in the source, indicating that the county’s rate is the operative benchmark). With a median annual tax bill of $3,425 on a median home value of $150,400, the jurisdiction can be classified as a high‑tax area relative to both national and state contexts. Property‑tax payments consume 5.10 % of the median household income of $67,159. This share is substantially higher than the typical 1–2 % range observed in many U.S. metros, suggesting a notable burden on household budgets. While the percentage remains below the threshold that would trigger severe affordability stress (often cited around 7–10 % of income), the cumulative effect is significant: over a 30‑year ownership horizon, a median‑priced home would generate $102,750 in property‑tax payments, assuming the current rate and tax bill remain unchanged. Prospective buyers should factor the $3,425 annual tax expense into cash‑flow calculations and compare it with mortgage‑payment capacity, especially given the modest median home price of $150,400. Buyers with tighter budgets may benefit from targeting properties in lower‑assessment districts or exploring homes with qualifying exemptions that reduce taxable value. Current owners can investigate general relief programs such as the Illinois Senior Citizens Homestead Exemption, the Homestead Improvement Exemption for energy‑efficiency upgrades, or the Agricultural Use Assessment for qualifying land, all of which can lower the effective rate. A less obvious insight emerges when the tax burden is expressed as a proportion of home value: the $3,425 annual levy represents roughly 2.28 % of the median home price, mirroring the effective tax rate. This alignment indicates that the county’s assessment practices produce a tax bill that scales directly with market value, leaving little room for “tax‑friendly” pockets within the county and reinforcing the importance of property‑value awareness for both buyers and existing homeowners.
