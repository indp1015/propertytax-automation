📝 Property Tax Notes

Woodruff County presents a distinctly low-tax environment relative to both state and national benchmarks. The county's effective property tax rate of 0.56% sits 36 basis points below the national average of 0.92% and exceeds the reported state average by 56 basis points. At $600 annually, the median property tax obligation is approximately 63% lower than the national median of $1,630, positioning Woodruff County among the more affordable property tax jurisdictions in the country. The affordability profile remains favorable when measured against local income levels. Property taxes consume 1.25% of median household income ($48,158), a ratio that suggests sustainable carrying costs for the typical homeowner. Over a 30-year ownership horizon, the cumulative tax burden on a median-valued home ($106,500) totals approximately $18,000 in current dollars—roughly equivalent to 17% of the home's present value. This long-term cost profile compares favorably to higher-rate jurisdictions where 30-year tax burdens frequently exceed 25-30% of initial home value. Prospective buyers should recognize that the low statutory rate operates against a modest assessment base; the median home value of $106,500 indicates limited inventory at higher price points where absolute tax savings compound more significantly. Current owners may benefit from reviewing eligibility for Arkansas homestead credits and age-65 assessment freezes, which can further reduce effective liability. A notable insight: the county's tax rate premium over the state average (0.56% vs. 0.00%) likely reflects minimal commercial and industrial tax base, shifting a disproportionate share of local government funding onto residential properties despite the low absolute rate.
