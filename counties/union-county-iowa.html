Property Tax in Union County, Iowa

In Union County, Iowa, the median property tax is $2,197/year — an effective tax rate of 1.8%. The median home value is $122,000.

Market Analysis

Union County's effective property tax rate of 1.8% is 37.1% above the Iowa state average and 96.5% above the U.S. national average. At this rate, a homeowner with a $400,000 property would pay approximately $7,200 per year in property taxes.
