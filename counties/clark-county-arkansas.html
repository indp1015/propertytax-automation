The median annual tax obligation of $871 is approximately 47% lower than the national median of $1,630, reflecting both the modest rate and a median home value of $155,000 that sits well below national levels. Affordability metrics indicate a manageable burden for median-income households. However, this figure masks significant variation across income brackets; lower-income homeowners may experience disproportionate pressure. A critical insight: the county's low effective rate combined with relatively low home values means that property tax savings versus higher-rate jurisdictions are modest in absolute dollar terms—approximately $759 annually versus the national median—potentially limiting the tax-driven financial advantage for relocation decisions.
