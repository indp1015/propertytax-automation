This share exceeds the typical national range of 1‑3 % for property‑tax‑to‑income ratios, indicating that while the rate is low, the high property values in the county drive a larger absolute tax burden. For most households earning the median income, allocating nearly five percent of earnings to property tax remains financially manageable, but it does reduce discretionary cash flow compared with lower‑tax regions. Prospective buyers should therefore factor this multi‑decadal tax commitment into affordability calculations, recognizing that the upfront purchase price is already elevated relative to many markets. Participation in local assessment appeals or qualifying for agricultural or historic preservation classifications may also yield modest savings, though eligibility varies. **One concrete insight:** the disparity between the low effective rate (0.50 %) and the high median tax bill ($5,828) is driven primarily by Dukes County’s **exceptionally high median home value**—the second‑highest in the state.
