How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your home value — current market value or recent purchase price
  2. Select your state and county — the calculator applies the local effective tax rate
  3. Adjust the assessment ratio — if your state assesses below 100% of market value
  4. Subtract your exemption — homestead, senior, or veteran exemptions
