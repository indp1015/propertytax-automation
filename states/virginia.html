Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in Virginia?

The average effective property tax rate in Virginia is 0.65%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 133 counties.

Which county in Virginia has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in Virginia is in Amelia County at 0.38%.

Which county has the highest property tax in Virginia?

The highest effective rate is in Manassas Park city at 1.17%.
