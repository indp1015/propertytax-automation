Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in Wisconsin?

The average effective property tax rate in Wisconsin is 1.36%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 72 counties.

Which county in Wisconsin has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in Wisconsin is in Vilas County at 0.73%.

Which county has the highest property tax in Wisconsin?

The highest effective rate is in Menominee County at 3.56%.
