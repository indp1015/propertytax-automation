About Massachusetts Property Taxes

Massachusetts has 14 counties, each with its own property tax rate set by local taxing authorities. The state's average effective rate of 1.03% is above the national average of 0.92%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in Massachusetts?

The average effective property tax rate in Massachusetts is 1.03%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 14 counties.

Which county in Massachusetts has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in Massachusetts is in Nantucket County at 0.25%.

Which county has the highest property tax in Massachusetts?

The highest effective rate is in Hampden County at 1.49%.
