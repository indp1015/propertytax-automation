Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in New Hampshire?

The average effective property tax rate in New Hampshire is 1.71%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 10 counties.

Which county in New Hampshire has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in New Hampshire is in Carroll County at 0.99%.

Which county has the highest property tax in New Hampshire?

The highest effective rate is in Cheshire County at 2.16%.
