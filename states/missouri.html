About Missouri Property Taxes

Missouri has 115 counties, each with its own property tax rate set by local taxing authorities. The state's average effective rate of 0.70% is below the national average of 0.92%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in Missouri?

The average effective property tax rate in Missouri is 0.70%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 115 counties.

Which county in Missouri has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in Missouri is in Reynolds County at 0.37%.

Which county has the highest property tax in Missouri?

The highest effective rate is in St. Louis County at 1.20%.
