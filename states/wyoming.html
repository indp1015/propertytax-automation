About Wyoming Property Taxes

Wyoming has 23 counties, each with its own property tax rate set by local taxing authorities. The state's average effective rate of 0.56% is below the national average of 0.92%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in Wyoming?

The average effective property tax rate in Wyoming is 0.56%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 23 counties.

Which county in Wyoming has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in Wyoming is in Sublette County at 0.44%.

Which county has the highest property tax in Wyoming?

The highest effective rate is in Weston County at 0.68%.
