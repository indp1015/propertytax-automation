About Nevada Property Taxes

Nevada has 17 counties, each with its own property tax rate set by local taxing authorities. The state's average effective rate of 0.52% is below the national average of 0.92%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in Nevada?

The average effective property tax rate in Nevada is 0.52%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 17 counties.

Which county in Nevada has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in Nevada is in Storey County at 0.34%.

Which county has the highest property tax in Nevada?

The highest effective rate is in Mineral County at 0.74%.
