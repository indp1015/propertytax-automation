About Idaho Property Taxes

Idaho has 44 counties, each with its own property tax rate set by local taxing authorities. The state's average effective rate of 0.46% is below the national average of 0.92%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in Idaho?

The average effective property tax rate in Idaho is 0.46%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 44 counties.

Which county in Idaho has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in Idaho is in Custer County at 0.29%.

Which county has the highest property tax in Idaho?

The highest effective rate is in Nez Perce County at 0.78%.
