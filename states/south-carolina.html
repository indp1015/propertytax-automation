Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in South Carolina?

The average effective property tax rate in South Carolina is 0.52%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 46 counties.

Which county in South Carolina has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in South Carolina is in Horry County at 0.33%.

Which county has the highest property tax in South Carolina?

The highest effective rate is in Allendale County at 0.88%.
