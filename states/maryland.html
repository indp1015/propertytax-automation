About Maryland Property Taxes

Maryland has 24 counties, each with its own property tax rate set by local taxing authorities. The state's average effective rate of 0.93% is above the national average of 0.92%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in Maryland?

The average effective property tax rate in Maryland is 0.93%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 24 counties.

Which county in Maryland has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in Maryland is in Talbot County at 0.65%.

Which county has the highest property tax in Maryland?

The highest effective rate is in Baltimore city at 1.46%.
