About Alabama Property Taxes

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in Alabama?

The average effective property tax rate in Alabama is 0.34%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 67 counties.

Which county in Alabama has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in Alabama is in Choctaw County at 0.17%.

Which county has the highest property tax in Alabama?

The highest effective rate is in Jefferson County at 0.59%.
