About Montana Property Taxes

Montana has 56 counties, each with its own property tax rate set by local taxing authorities. The state's average effective rate of 0.73% is below the national average of 0.92%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in Montana?

The average effective property tax rate in Montana is 0.73%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 56 counties.

Which county in Montana has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in Montana is in Madison County at 0.41%.

Which county has the highest property tax in Montana?

The highest effective rate is in Blaine County at 1.34%.
