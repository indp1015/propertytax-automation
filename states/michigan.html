About Michigan Property Taxes

Michigan has 83 counties, each with its own property tax rate set by local taxing authorities. The state's average effective rate of 1.12% is above the national average of 0.92%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in Michigan?

The average effective property tax rate in Michigan is 1.12%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 83 counties.

Which county in Michigan has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in Michigan is in Leelanau County at 0.68%.

Which county has the highest property tax in Michigan?

The highest effective rate is in Ingham County at 1.83%.
