About California Property Taxes

California has 56 counties, each with its own property tax rate set by local taxing authorities. The state's average effective rate of 0.70% is below the national average of 0.92%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in California?

The average effective property tax rate in California is 0.70%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 56 counties.

Which county in California has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in California is in Trinity County at 0.58%.

Which county has the highest property tax in California?

The highest effective rate is in Kern County at 0.88%.
