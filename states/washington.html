About Washington Property Taxes

Washington has 39 counties, each with its own property tax rate set by local taxing authorities. The state's average effective rate of 0.73% is below the national average of 0.92%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in Washington?

The average effective property tax rate in Washington is 0.73%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 39 counties.

Which county in Washington has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in Washington is in San Juan County at 0.53%.

Which county has the highest property tax in Washington?

The highest effective rate is in Pierce County at 0.91%.
