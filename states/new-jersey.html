About New Jersey Property Taxes

New Jersey has 21 counties, each with its own property tax rate set by local taxing authorities. The state's average effective rate of 2.08% is above the national average of 0.92%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in New Jersey?

The average effective property tax rate in New Jersey is 2.08%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 21 counties.

Which county in New Jersey has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in New Jersey is in Cape May County at 1.22%.

Which county has the highest property tax in New Jersey?

The highest effective rate is in Salem County at 2.93%.
