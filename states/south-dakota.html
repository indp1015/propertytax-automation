About South Dakota Property Taxes

South Dakota has 66 counties, each with its own property tax rate set by local taxing authorities. The state's average effective rate of 1.03% is above the national average of 0.92%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in South Dakota?

The average effective property tax rate in South Dakota is 1.03%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 66 counties.

Which county in South Dakota has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in South Dakota is in Oglala Lakota County at 0.38%.

Which county has the highest property tax in South Dakota?

The highest effective rate is in Todd County at 1.81%.
