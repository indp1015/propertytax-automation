About Louisiana Property Taxes

Louisiana has 64 counties, each with its own property tax rate set by local taxing authorities. The state's average effective rate of 0.43% is below the national average of 0.92%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in Louisiana?

The average effective property tax rate in Louisiana is 0.43%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 64 counties.

Which county in Louisiana has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in Louisiana is in East Feliciana Parish at 0.15%.

Which county has the highest property tax in Louisiana?

The highest effective rate is in Orleans Parish at 0.83%.
