About Connecticut Property Taxes

Connecticut has 9 counties, each with its own property tax rate set by local taxing authorities. The state's average effective rate of 1.73% is above the national average of 0.92%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in Connecticut?

The average effective property tax rate in Connecticut is 1.73%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 9 counties.

Which county in Connecticut has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in Connecticut is in Northeastern Connecticut Planning Region at 1.42%.

Which county has the highest property tax in Connecticut?

The highest effective rate is in Greater Bridgeport Planning Region at 2.02%.
