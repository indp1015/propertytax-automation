Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in Colorado?

The average effective property tax rate in Colorado is 0.39%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 64 counties.

Which county in Colorado has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in Colorado is in Jackson County at 0.17%.

Which county has the highest property tax in Colorado?
