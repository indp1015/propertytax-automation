About Vermont Property Taxes

Vermont has 14 counties, each with its own property tax rate set by local taxing authorities. The state's average effective rate of 1.65% is above the national average of 0.92%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in Vermont?

The average effective property tax rate in Vermont is 1.65%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 14 counties.

Which county in Vermont has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in Vermont is in Grand Isle County at 1.32%.

Which county has the highest property tax in Vermont?

The highest effective rate is in Windsor County at 1.84%.
