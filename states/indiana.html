About Indiana Property Taxes

Indiana has 92 counties, each with its own property tax rate set by local taxing authorities. The state's average effective rate of 0.66% is below the national average of 0.92%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in Indiana?

The average effective property tax rate in Indiana is 0.66%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 92 counties.

Which county in Indiana has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in Indiana is in Clay County at 0.41%.

Which county has the highest property tax in Indiana?

The highest effective rate is in Lake County at 0.92%.
