About Maine Property Taxes

Maine has 16 counties, each with its own property tax rate set by local taxing authorities. The state's average effective rate of 1.03% is above the national average of 0.92%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in Maine?

The average effective property tax rate in Maine is 1.03%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 16 counties.

Which county in Maine has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in Maine is in Hancock County at 0.83%.

Which county has the highest property tax in Maine?

The highest effective rate is in Knox County at 1.17%.
