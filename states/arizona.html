About Arizona Property Taxes

Arizona has 15 counties, each with its own property tax rate set by local taxing authorities. The state's average effective rate of 0.55% is below the national average of 0.92%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in Arizona?

The average effective property tax rate in Arizona is 0.55%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 15 counties.

Which county in Arizona has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in Arizona is in Greenlee County at 0.33%.

Which county has the highest property tax in Arizona?

The highest effective rate is in Apache County at 0.90%.
