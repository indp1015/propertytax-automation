About Kansas Property Taxes

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in Kansas?

The average effective property tax rate in Kansas is 1.54%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 105 counties.

Which county in Kansas has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in Kansas is in Nemaha County at 0.96%.

Which county has the highest property tax in Kansas?

The highest effective rate is in Stanton County at 2.46%.
