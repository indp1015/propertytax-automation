Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in North Carolina?

The average effective property tax rate in North Carolina is 0.67%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 100 counties.

Which county in North Carolina has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in North Carolina is in Jackson County at 0.34%.

Which county has the highest property tax in North Carolina?

The highest effective rate is in Halifax County at 1.15%.
