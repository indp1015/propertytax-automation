About Utah Property Taxes

Utah has 29 counties, each with its own property tax rate set by local taxing authorities. The state's average effective rate of 0.48% is below the national average of 0.92%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in Utah?

The average effective property tax rate in Utah is 0.48%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 29 counties.

Which county in Utah has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in Utah is in Rich County at 0.26%.

Which county has the highest property tax in Utah?

The highest effective rate is in San Juan County at 0.80%.
