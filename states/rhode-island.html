Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in Rhode Island?

The average effective property tax rate in Rhode Island is 1.16%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 5 counties.

Which county in Rhode Island has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in Rhode Island is in Newport County at 0.89%.

Which county has the highest property tax in Rhode Island?

The highest effective rate is in Kent County at 1.35%.
