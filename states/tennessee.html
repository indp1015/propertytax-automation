Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in Tennessee?

The average effective property tax rate in Tennessee is 0.48%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 95 counties.

Which county in Tennessee has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in Tennessee is in Sevier County at 0.28%.

Which county has the highest property tax in Tennessee?

The highest effective rate is in Shelby County at 0.98%.
