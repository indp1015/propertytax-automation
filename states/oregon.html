About Oregon Property Taxes

Oregon has 36 counties, each with its own property tax rate set by local taxing authorities. The state's average effective rate of 0.74% is below the national average of 0.92%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average property tax rate in Oregon?

The average effective property tax rate in Oregon is 0.74%, based on the median property tax paid divided by median home value across all 36 counties.

Which county in Oregon has the lowest property tax?

The lowest effective rate in Oregon is in Curry County at 0.48%.

Which county has the highest property tax in Oregon?

The highest effective rate is in Gilliam County at 1.01%.
